# Privacy Policy - CashBurst

**Last Updated:** May 2026

## 1. Introduction

CashBurst ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- Name, email address, phone number
- KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN card details)
- Bank account details and UPI IDs
- Device information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers)

### 2.2 Usage Data
- Ad watch history and completion status
- Transaction history
- Login timestamps and activity patterns
- Referral activity
- IP address and approximate location

### 2.3 Financial Information
- Wallet balance and earnings
- Withdrawal history and payment details
- Bank account information for payouts

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain our service
- To process withdrawals and payments
- To verify user identity (KYC compliance)
- To prevent fraud and ensure security
- To improve user experience
- To send notifications about rewards and updates
- To comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- Secure JWT-based authentication
- Encrypted storage of financial information
- Regular security audits
- Device fingerprinting for fraud prevention
- VPN and emulator detection

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:
- Payment processors (Razorpay, Cashfree, Paytm) for withdrawals
- KYC verification services
- Law enforcement when legally required
- Service providers who assist our operations

## 6. User Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Request data portability

## 7. Data Retention

We retain user data for as long as your account is active. Financial transaction records are kept for 7 years as per Indian regulatory requirements.

## 8. Children's Privacy

CashBurst is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes through the app.
